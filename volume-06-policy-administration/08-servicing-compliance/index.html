    6.8 Servicing & Compliance¶

    Overview¶

    Servicing covers the day-to-day interaction with policyholders, producers, and regulators that keeps the policy accurate and the relationship healthy. Compliance ensures those activities meet legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.

    Servicing Activities¶

    Activity Description
    Customer service Answers, document requests, policy questions
    Certificates & evidence Certificates of insurance, letters of good standing
    Billing inquiries Payment status, plans, adjustments
    Endorsement requests Changes, additions, removals
    Renewal assistance Quotes, terms, explanations
    Claims coordination Notices, documentation, handoff to claims
    Producer support Agency questions, quotes, binding authority
    Record updates Address, contact, payment changes

    Service Standards¶

    Standard Example
    Response time Phone/email resolution within SLAs
    Accuracy Correct policy information on first call
    Empathy Professional and respectful communication
    Resolution Complete answers, no follow-up needed
    Documentation Call notes and records retained

    Certificates of Insurance¶

    • Purpose — evidence that coverage exists; not a contract change.
    • Issuance — certificate holder, insured, limits, period, insurer.
    • Accuracy — verify requested coverage exists before issuing.
    • Additional insured status — only if endorsed on the policy.
    • Control — certificates must not misstate coverage.

    Regulatory Compliance¶

    Area Key Rules
    Cancellation notices Statutory timing and content
    Non-renewal notices Notice period; reasons where required
    Adverse action FCRA notice for credit-based decisions
    Privacy GLBA/GDPR — data protection and use
    Records retention Regulatory minimums
    Market conduct Fair treatment, no misrepresentation
    Producer licensing Appointments, commissions, authority

    See 1.5 The Legal Environment.

    Market Conduct¶

    Market conduct reviews assess whether insurers treat consumers fairly:

    • Complaint handling — timely, fair resolution.
    • Sales practices — no misrepresentation, suitability.
    • Advertising — truthful, not misleading.
    • Claims practices — timely, accurate (Volume 7).
    • Underwriting practices — no unfair discrimination.

    Data Protection and Privacy¶

    Requirement Practice
    Lawful basis Process data only with basis (consent, contract, law)
    Minimization Collect only what is necessary
    Security Access controls, encryption, monitoring
    Breach response Notification where required
    Consumer rights Access, correction, erasure where applicable

    Recordkeeping¶

    Record Retention Driver
    Policy documents Contract period + claims statue + regulation
    Claims files Long-tail liability only
    Billing records Premium disputes, audits
    Correspondence Servicing and complaints
    Regulatory filings Permanent

    Retention schedules should be documented and enforced.

    Complaint Management¶

    Receive complaint → Log → Investigate → Respond → Remediate → Report
    Step Practice
    Log Central record; category, date, owner
    Investigate Gather policy, servicing, claims records
    Respond Clear, timely explanation; resolution offered
    Remediate Correct the issue; adjust policy/premium
    Report Internal and regulatory reporting

    Summary¶

    • Servicing keeps policies accurate and customers supported.
    • Certificates and evidence must not misstate coverage.
    • Compliance spans notices, privacy, market conduct, and records.
    • Market conduct and complaint management protect consumers and the insurer.
    • Documentation and retention support audits and disputes.
