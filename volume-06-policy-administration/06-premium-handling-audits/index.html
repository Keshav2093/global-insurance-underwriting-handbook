    6.6 Premium Handling & Audits¶

    Overview¶

    Premium handling covers the billing, collection, allocation, adjustment, and audit of premium. For many commercial lines, the final premium is not known until the end of the term because it is based on actual exposures (payroll, receipts, values) — hence the premium audit.

    Billing and Payment Plans¶

    Plan Description
    Annual premium Single payment at inception
    Installments Monthly/quarterly payments; service fees often apply
    Direct bill Insurer bills the insured
    Agency bill Producer bills and remits
    Payroll deduction Employer deducts from wages (life/health)
    Electronic funds ACH/card automatic payments

    The Premium Cycle¶

    Quote/Issue → Billing → Collection → Allocation → Reconciliation → Audit (if exposure-based) → Adjustment
    Stage Activity
    Billing Generate invoice with due dates
    Collection Receive payment; apply to policy
    Allocation Apply to the right policy/coverage
    Reconciliation Match receipts to invoices, agents, banks
    Audit Determine actual exposure at term end
    Adjustment Additional or return premium

    Payment and Non-Payment¶

    Event Handling
    Paid in full Policy effective; no outstanding balance
    Installment missed Grace period; late notice; cancellation exposure
    NSF/reversal Recover premium; reinstate or cancel
    Audit additional premium Bill after audit
    Return premium Refund or apply to other balances

    Non-payment procedures must follow policy terms and statutory requirements.

    The Premium Audit¶

    Premium audits verify the actual exposure base at the end of the policy period.

    Audit Type Description
    Physical audit On-site review of records
    Mail/desk audit Correspondence-based review
    Self-audit Insured completes the audit form; insurer verifies
    Estimated annual Interim estimate; final at term end

    Audit Basis by Line¶

    Line Audited Exposure
    Workers comp Payroll by class
    General liability Gross receipts (premises), sales (products)
    Commercial auto Mileage, fleet composition
    Property (reporting forms) Monthly values reported

    Audit Process¶

    1. Send audit request (at expiry or periodically)
2. Insured provides payroll/receipts/exposure records
3. Auditor verifies classification and amounts
4. Calculate adjusted premium
5. Issue additional premium (AP) bill or return premium (RP)
6. Document and record

    Classification Errors at Audit¶

    Error Impact
    Wrong class code Premium change; possible misreporting penalties
    Misstated payroll Incorrect premium; audit exposure
    Unreported locations Missing exposure
    Excluded operations performed Premium evasion

    The insured is obligated to report accurately; audits verify compliance.

    Reconciliation and Control¶

    Control Purpose
    Cash application Payments applied to correct policies
    Aging reports Track overdue balances
    Write-off authority Controlled bad debt
    Reconciliation Bank vs. ledger vs. agency balances
    Premium trust Producer funds held in trust where required
    Statutory accounting Premium recognition per accounting basis

    Return and Additional Premiums¶

    Type Trigger
    AP (additional premium) Endorsement, audit, rate correction
    RP (return premium) Cancellation, endorsement, audit reduction
    Short-rate Insured cancellation penalty
    Refund Overpayment or rescission

    All AP/RP must be documented and promptly processed.

    Summary¶

    • Premium handling covers billing, collection, allocation, reconciliation, and audit.
    • Premium audits verify actual exposures for many commercial lines.
    • Classification and reporting accuracy drive correct premium.
    • Non-payment follows policy and statutory procedures.
    • Controls and reconciliation protect the insurer's cash and records.
