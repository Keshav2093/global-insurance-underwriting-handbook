# 3.1 Commercial Lines Overview

## What Are Commercial Lines?

**Commercial lines** insure the exposures of **businesses, organizations, and professionals**. They encompass:

- **Commercial property** — buildings, contents, business income.
- **Commercial general liability (CGL)** — third-party bodily injury and property damage.
- **Commercial auto** — owned, hired, and non-owned vehicles.
- **Workers compensation** — statutory employee injury benefits.
- **Package policies** — combined property and liability (BOP, commercial package).
- **Specialty lines** — marine, aviation, surety, professional, cyber, D&O, E&O, environmental, and more.
- **Life, accident, and health** — group benefits and business protection.

## Commercial Market Segments

| Segment | Characteristics | Typical Need |
|---|---|---|
| **Small business** | Revenues under ~\$5M; few employees; simple exposures | BOP, small commercial package |
| **Middle market** | Revenues \$5M–\$500M; complex operations; risk manager or broker-managed | Customized package, layered limits |
| **Large / national accounts** | Revenues over \$500M; multi-location; captives and complex programs | Program business, self-insurance, bespoke structures |
| **Specialty** | High-risk, unusual, or hard-to-place | Non-admitted/surplus lines, London market, specific expertise |

## Distribution in Commercial Lines

- **Agents** — typically for small business and middle market.
- **Brokers** — dominant for middle market and larger; negotiate programs, market submissions.
- **MGAs and coverholders** — delegated underwriting authority for niche programs (e.g., trucking, restaurants, high-value property).
- **Direct** — growing for small business via digital platforms.

The broker's **submission** is the core information package for underwriting.

## The Submission Process

| Step | Description |
|---|---|
| **Broker/agent gathers data** | Applications, loss runs, financials, values, photos, engineering reports |
| **Submission to market** | The insurer reviews and may request additional data |
| **Underwriting analysis** | COPE, exposure assessment, experience review |
| **Indication/quote** | Preliminary indication or firm quote subject to conditions |
| **Binder / binding** | Coverage attaches; pending receipt of documents and premium |
| **Issuance** | Final policy documentation |

## Underwriting Data for Commercial Lines

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| **Applications** | ACORD applications by line (property, GL, auto, WC) |
| **Loss runs** | Prior carrier loss history (typically 3–5 years) |
| **Financial statements** | Business financials; credit report; bankruptcy search |
| **Values** | Statement of values (SOV), property schedules |
| **Inspections** | Risk engineering reports, photos |
| **COPE data** | Construction, occupancy, protection, exposure |
| **MVRs** | For commercial auto |
| **Class codes / payroll** | For workers compensation |
| **Contracts** | Lease, purchase, hold-harmless agreements |

## Package vs. Mono-Line

| Structure | Description |
|---|---|
| **Mono-line** | Separate policy for each coverage (property, GL, auto, WC) |
| **Package (BOP)** | Simplified combined form for small businesses; property + liability + selected extras in one policy |
| **Commercial Package Policy (CPP)** | Two or more coverage parts in one policy, with common declarations and conditions |

Packaging benefits:

- Fewer documents and easier compliance.
- Interline discounts and credits.
- One renewal and service relationship.

## The Underwriting Cycle in Commercial Lines

Commercial lines are subject to the same **underwriting cycle** as personal lines, but amplified for larger risks:

- **Soft market** — broad terms, low rates, high capacity.
- **Hard market** — reduced capacity, higher rates, reduced limits, more exclusions.

Large commercial accounts are individually negotiated; terms vary by account. **Portfolio management** matters: concentration by territory, class, and catastrophe exposure.

## Regulation of Commercial Lines

- **US:** state rate and form regulation; large commercial accounts often exempt from rate filing (file-and-use/manual exceptions); surplus lines market for non-admitted.
- **UK:** FCA/PRA; Solvency II; fairness and consumer duty apply but corporate customers have fewer conduct protections than consumers.
- **Cross-border:** global programs require local policies (admitted) plus a master policy (non-admitted or London).

## Role of the Commercial Underwriter

The commercial underwriter:

1. **Assesses** the risk — exposures, hazards, controls, management quality.
2. **Selects** — accepts, declines, or modifies.
3. **Prices** — using manual rates, experience rating, and judgment.
4. **Sets terms** — limits, deductibles, exclusions, warranties.
5. **Documents** — the file rationale.
6. **Manages** — the portfolio and the account at renewal.

## Summary

- Commercial lines cover **business and professional risks** across property, liability, auto, WC, package, and specialty.
- The **broker's submission** and **data quality** drive underwriting.
- **Packages** simplify small business coverage; **large accounts** require bespoke structures.
- The **cycle**, **portfolio management**, and **regulation** shape commercial underwriting.
