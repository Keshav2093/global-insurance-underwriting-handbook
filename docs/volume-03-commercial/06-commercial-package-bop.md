# 3.6 Commercial Package & BOP

## Overview

Many businesses need more than one coverage. **Commercial packages** combine property and liability coverages under one policy, and the **Businessowners Policy (BOP)** is a simplified package designed for small businesses.

| Structure | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **BOP** | Simplified, standardized package: property + liability + selected extras in one form | Small businesses (retail, offices, apartments, small contractors) |
| **Commercial Package Policy (CPP)** | Two or more coverage parts (property, GL, auto, crime, inland marine) in one policy with common declarations and conditions | Middle market businesses needing flexible, customized coverages |

## The Commercial Package Policy (CPP)

### Components

The CPP (ISO) consists of:

- **Common Declarations** — insured, policy period, locations.
- **Common Policy Conditions** — cancellation, examination of books, changes, etc.
- **Coverage Parts** — one or more of:
  - Commercial Property
  - Commercial General Liability
  - Commercial Crime
  - Boiler & Machinery (Equipment Breakdown)
  - Inland Marine
  - Commercial Auto
  - Farm (in some programs)
- **Supplemental Declarations and Forms** — per coverage part.

### Interline Conditions

- **Liberalization** — coverage automatically broadened if forms are revised.
- **Non-renewal conditions** — common notice requirements.
- **Policy period and territory** — common to all parts.

### Advantages of Packaging

- One policy document, one premium, one renewal.
- **Interline discounts** — premium credits for combining coverages.
- **Consistent terms and conditions** — gaps between coverage parts reduced.
- **Simpler administration** — for both insurer and insured.

## The Businessowners Policy (BOP)

The **BOP** (ISO BP 00 03) is designed for small businesses that meet eligibility criteria. It provides a broad, standardized set of coverages:

### Section I — Property

- **Buildings** and **business personal property** at described premises.
- **Business income and extra expense** (included, not optional).
- **Perils** — open perils (special) for covered property (with exclusions).
- **Valuation** — replacement cost on buildings and most contents.
- **Included extras** — debris removal, fire department charges, pollutant cleanup, etc.
- **Optional coverages** — outdoor property, glass, signs, spoilage, etc.

### Section II — Liability

- **Bodily injury and property damage liability** (occurrence-based).
- **Personal and advertising injury**.
- **Medical payments**.
- **Defense costs** (outside limits).

### Other Coverages

- **Loss of income** — included.
- **Crime** — optional (employee dishonesty, theft of money/securities).
- **Equipment breakdown** — optional.
- **Hired/non-owned auto** — optional.
- **Umbrella/excess** — optional BOP umbrella.

### BOP Eligibility

| Eligible | Not Eligible |
|---|---|
| Retail/wholesale (non-high-risk) | Manufacturing with significant hazards |
| Offices (non-professional services) | Professional services (law, medicine, accounting) |
| Apartments (limited units) | High-risk occupancies (bars, nightclubs) |
| Small contractors (limits on size) | Auto/trucking operations |
| Service businesses (low hazard) | Hazardous materials |
| Restaurants (with conditions) | Large, complex risks |

Eligibility is defined by **class/occupancy** and **size** (square footage, receipts, employees). The BOP is not available for risks that require special forms or high limits.

### BOP vs. CPP

| Factor | BOP | CPP |
|---|---|---|
| Forms | Standardized | Customizable |
| Eligibility | Strict occupancy/size limits | Any qualifying commercial risk |
| Property | Open perils, business income included | As desired (basic/broad/special; BI optional) |
| Liability | Occurrence only | Occurrence or claims-made |
| Pricing | Manual rates, simplified | Manual + judgment + experience |
| Best for | Small, simple risks | Larger or unusual risks |

## Underwriting the Package

### Interline Underwriting

The package should be underwritten as a **whole**, not line by line:

- Check **consistency** — limits, premises, operations across coverage parts.
- Verify **eligibility** for BOP vs. CPP choice.
- Assess **cross-line hazards** — a restaurant has property (grease, fire), GL (slip/trip, food liability), and crime exposures.
- Manage **interline credits** — packaging discounts are justified by combined economics.

### BOP Underwriting Checklist

- Eligibility (occupancy, size).
- Property values and condition (COPE).
- Business income adequacy.
- Liability exposures (premises, operations, products).
- Hired/non-owned auto needs.
- Crime exposure (cash handling, employees).
- Prior losses (frequency/severity).
- Management quality and housekeeping.

## Rating the Package

| Factor | BOP | CPP |
|---|---|---|
| **Property** | Rating by occupancy, construction, protection, territory | Manual plus judgment |
| **Liability** | Based on receipts, payroll, square footage | Class rates on exposure bases |
| **Packaging discount** | Included in BOP pricing | Interline credits applied |
| **Experience** | Limited or none | Experience rating available |
| **Minimum premium** | Fixed per class | As developed |

## UK Equivalent

- **Commercial combined** policies bundle property, contents, business interruption, public/products liability, and employers' liability.
- **Shop, office, and trade policies** are standard small-business products.
- Ratings typically based on **sum insured, class of trade, and claims experience**.
- **Average clauses** apply to property sections.

## Summary

- The **BOP** is a standardized small-business package; the **CPP** is a flexible multi-part package.
- **Eligibility** governs which product applies.
- Packages are underwritten **holistically** across lines.
- **Interline discounts** and simplified terms drive package economics.
