# 3.7 Businessowners Policy (BOP)

## Overview

The **Businessowners Policy (BOP)** is a simplified package for small businesses. It combines property, business income, and liability coverage into one standardized form, with optional coverages available by endorsement.

This chapter deepens the BOP discussion from [3.6](06-commercial-package-bop.md), focusing on **rating, endorsements, underwriting detail, and claims considerations**.

## BOP Structure

The ISO BOP (BP 00 03) is built around:

- **Eligibility** — occupancy class and size criteria.
- **Section I — Property** (Building, Business Personal Property, Business Income/Extra Expense).
- **Section II — Liability** (BI/PD, Personal & Advertising Injury, Medical Payments).
- **Conditions and Definitions** common to both sections.
- **Endorsements** — adding coverages as needed.

### Coverage Summary

| Coverage | Included? |
|---|---|
| Buildings | Only if elected (property limit) |
| Business personal property | Included |
| Business income / extra expense | Included |
| Outdoor property & signs | Limited, included |
| Glass | Included as part of property |
| Crime (employee dishonesty, theft of money/securities) | Option |
| Equipment breakdown | Option |
| Hired/non-owned auto liability | Option |
| Professional liability | Option (limited classes) |
| Umbrella / excess | Option |

## BOP Rating

The BOP is rated using **class/occupancy rates** rather than pure manual property and liability components:

| Component | Rating Basis |
|---|---|
| **Property** | Building value, contents value, construction, protection, territory |
| **Business income** | Based on property values and class factors |
| **Liability** | Receipts, payroll, square footage, number of apartments/units |
| **Optional coverages** | Flat charges or stated exposure bases |
| **Deductibles** | Choice of general/GL deductible; percentage wind/hail deductible |
| **Minimum premium** | Fixed by class/territory |

### Rating Example (Illustrative)

- Class: 5,000 sq ft retail store; contents \$250,000; no building owned.
- Base liability premium from class rate × exposure.
- Contents premium: \$250,000 × rate per \$1,000 at 80% coinsurance.
- Business income: factor on contents limit.
- Optional crime: flat charge per location.
- Deductible: \$1,000 property; \$500 GL.
- **Result:** annual premium with BOP pricing already including package credits.

## Underwriting the BOP

### Core Underwriting Steps

1. **Eligibility check** — occupancy, size, receipts; ensure the risk qualifies.
2. **Property values** — verify building/contents values; replacement cost adequacy; coinsurance.
3. **COPE analysis** — construction, occupancy, protection, exposure.
4. **Liability review** — premises conditions, operations, products, contracts.
5. **Business income** — verify revenue and recovery time.
6. **Loss history** — 3–5 years loss runs.
7. **Management/housekeeping** — visit or inspection where indicated.
8. **Optional coverages** — quote and endorse; ensure no gaps.

### Key BOP Underwriting Questions

| Question | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Is the occupancy eligible? | BOP eligibility controls |
| Are values current and adequate? | Coinsurance and adequacy |
| Is there a significant products exposure? | BOP liability limits may be insufficient |
| Are there hired/non-owned autos? | Add coverage if employees drive on business |
| Is there a cash/asset handling risk? | Crime coverage need |
| Are there special hazards (restaurant cooking, flammable storage)? | Endorsements or decline |
| Is there a business interruption exposure? | BI adequacy; verify revenue |

### BOP Underwriting Red Flags

- Underinsured values (values below coinsurance requirement).
- Ineligible occupancy or size.
- Poor housekeeping; fire hazards.
- Claims frequency (small claims pattern).
- Products/completed operations needing higher limits than BOP offers.
- Multi-vehicle operations (BOP auto coverage is limited).
- Professional services that require separate E&O.

## BOP Endorsements (Common)

| Endorsement | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Ordinance or Law** | Adds code upgrade coverage |
| **Spoilage** | Refrigerated stock |
| **Money and Securities (Crime)** | Employee dishonesty, theft of money/securities |
| **Equipment Breakdown** | Mechanical/electrical |
| **Hired/Non-Owned Auto** | Auto liability for hired/non-owned |
| **Umbrella/Excess** | Higher limits |
| **Professional Liability** | Adds specific professional coverage (limited) |
| **Employment Practices** | Adds EPLI |
| **Cyber** | Adds data breach |
| **Flood / Earthquake** | Adds CAT perils |
| **Additional Insured** | Endorses additional parties |

## BOP Claims Considerations

- **Prompt notice** — the BOP requires prompt notice of loss; failure may prejudice coverage.
- **Duties after loss** — protect property, cooperate, submit sworn statement, allow inspection.
- **Valuation** — replacement cost (buildings and contents) subject to coinsurance compliance.
- **Business income** — actual loss sustained during the period of restoration.
- **Reservation of rights / coverage questions** — eligibility and occupancy misrepresentation can void BOP coverage.

## When a BOP Is Not the Right Product

| Situation | Preferred Solution |
|---|---|
| Large or complex exposure | CPP or specialty |
| High-risk occupancy (bars, nightclubs) | Specialty program or surplus lines |
| Manufacturing with major hazards | CPP with tailored protections |
| Professional practice (law, medicine, accounting) | Professional liability + CPP |
| Trucking/fleet operations | Commercial auto + cargo + GL |
| High limits (>\$1M–\$2M GL) | CPP + umbrella |

## Summary

- The BOP is a **standardized small-business package** with property, BI, and liability included.
- Rating is **class-based** with package credits built in.
- Underwriting centers on **eligibility, values, COPE, loss history, and optional coverage needs**.
- **Endorsements** extend the BOP; some exposures require moving to a CPP or specialty market.
