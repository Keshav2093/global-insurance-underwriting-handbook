# Volume 3 — Commercial Insurance

Volume 3 covers insurance for businesses and organizations. It explains commercial property, general liability, commercial auto, workers compensation, and the other commercial products that underwriters encounter, from the perspective of the underwriting function: what is covered, what drives risk, and what underwriting and rating considerations apply.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this volume you should be able to:

1. Describe the commercial lines marketplace and the submission process.
2. Explain commercial property coverages, valuation, and coinsurance.
3. Explain the CGL structure and its coverage triggers.
4. Identify commercial auto symbols and trucking underwriting considerations.
5. Explain the workers compensation system, class codes, and experience rating.
6. Distinguish the BOP from a full commercial package.
7. Describe life, health, and disability products for businesses.
8. Recognize specialty lines and their unique underwriting requirements.
9. Describe how risk managers structure commercial insurance programs.
