# 9.2 UK Regulation

## Overview

The UK uses a **twin peaks** regulatory model. The **Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA)** supervises financial safety and soundness; the **Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)** regulates conduct of business and market integrity. **Solvency II** (with UK adaptations) provides the capital framework.

## The Twin Peaks Model

| Regulator | Focus | Key Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| **PRA** (Bank of England) | Prudential | Solvency, capital, risk management, governance |
| **FCA** | Conduct | Fair treatment, market integrity, consumer protection |

Insurers are **dual-regulated**; intermediaries (brokers, MGAs) are FCA-only.

## Solvency II (UK)

| Pillar | Content |
|---|---|
| **Pillar 1** | Capital requirements — SCR, MCR, valuation of assets/liabilities |
| **Pillar 2** | Governance, ORSA, risk management |
| **Pillar 3** | Disclosure — SFCR, RSR, regulatory reporting |

The UK maintains Solvency II with **post-Brexit adaptations** (e.g., reforms to the matching adjustment and internal models).

## FCA Conduct Rules

| Rulebook Area | Content |
|---|---|
| **Principles for Businesses** | 12 high-level principles (integrity, skill, customers, communication) |
| **COBS** | Conduct of Business Sourcebook — selling, advice, disclosure |
| **ICOBS** | Insurance Conduct of Business Sourcebook |
| **PRIN** | Principles for Businesses |
| **Consumer Duty** | Fair value, consumer understanding, support, outcomes |

## Consumer Duty (Implementation since 2023)

The **Consumer Duty** requires firms to act to deliver good outcomes for retail customers:

| Cross-cutting rules | Actions |
|---|---|
| **Act in good faith** | Toward retail customers |
| **Avoid foreseeable harm** | Take reasonable care |
| **Enable customers to pursue financial objectives** | Act in their interests |

Four outcomes: **products and services, price and value, consumer understanding, consumer support.**

## Lloyd's Regulation

| Layer | Role |
|---|---|
| **PRA/FCA** | Authorize Lloyd's and its members/agents |
| **Lloyd's Corporation** | Sets market standards, audits syndicates |
| **Society of Lloyd's** | Governs the market |

The Lloyd's market operates within the UK regulatory framework with additional market-specific rules.

## Intermediary Regulation

| Requirement | Description |
|---|---|
| **FCA authorization** | Brokers, MGAs, agents must be authorized or appointed representatives |
| **Financial crime compliance** | AML/CFT controls |
| **Client money** | Safeguarding requirements |
| **Professional indemnity** | Mandatory PI cover |
| **Conduct rules** | IDD implementation — transparency and suitability |

## The Insurance Distribution Directive (IDD)

IDD (transposed into UK rules) requires:

- **Professional requirements** — knowledge and ability of staff.
- **Disclosure** — status, remuneration, conflicts.
- **Product governance** — target market and oversight.
- **Suitability/appropriateness** where advice is given.

## Prudential Standards

| Standard | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Own funds** | Capital resources quality and quantity |
| **SCR/MCR** | Solvency capital requirement, minimum capital requirement |
| **ORSA** | Own Risk and Solvency Assessment |
| **Governance** | Fit and proper, risk functions, outsourcing |
| **Supervisory review** | PRA supervisory engagement and stress tests |

## Enforcement

| Tool | Example |
|---|---|
| **Fines** | Penalties for misconduct |
| **Public censure** | Reputation and reporting |
| **Prohibition orders** | Ban individuals from functions |
| **Variation of permissions** | Restrict business |
| **Skilled person reviews** | Independent reviews (s166) |

## The Financial Ombudsman Service (FOS)

- Resolves **consumer complaints** against firms.
- Free to consumers; binding on firms.
- Awards up to statutory limits.

## The Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS)

- Protects consumers if a firm **fails**.
- Covers insurance policies, investments, and deposits.
- Funded by **levies** on firms.

## Summary

- **PRA** supervises prudential soundness; **FCA** regulates conduct.
- **Solvency II (UK)** sets capital and governance standards.
- **Consumer Duty** requires good outcomes for retail customers.
- **Lloyd's** operates under PRA/FCA plus its own market supervision.
- **FOS and FSCS** protect consumers in disputes and firm failure.
