# 9.3 Forms & Contracts

## Overview

UK insurance contracts are governed by **English law** and general contract principles, with statutory overlay from the **Consumer Insurance (Disclosure and Representations) Act 2012 (CIDRA)** and the **Insurance Act 2015**. Market forms are less standardized than the US ISO system; the London market uses **slips, wordings, and clauses** developed by market bodies such as the **Lloyd's Market Association (LMA)**.

## Sources of UK Insurance Law

| Source | Role |
|---|---|
| **Common law** | Contract principles, precedent |
| **Marine Insurance Act 1906** | Codified general insurance law (still influential) |
| **Consumer Insurance Act 2012 (CIDRA)** | Consumer disclosure — reasonable care |
| **Insurance Act 2015** | Business insurance — fair presentation, remedies, warranties |
| **FCA rules** | Conduct requirements |
| **Market practice** | Lloyds and LMA standard wordings |

## The Insurance Act 2015 — Key Reforms

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| **Fair presentation** | Reasonable search; material circumstances or sufficient information |
| **Remedies for non-disclosure** | Proportionate — not automatic avoidance |
| **Warranties** | Suspensory — no longer discharge all liability |
| **Fraudulent claims** | Claims forfeit; policy may continue |
| **Damages for late payment** | Statutory right to damages |

The Act applies to **non-consumer** insurance.

## CIDRA — Consumer Contracts

| Requirement | Description |
|---|---|
| **Duty of reasonable care** | Consumer must take reasonable care in answers |
| **Qualifying misrepresentation** | Innocent, negligent, or deliberate/reckless |
| **Remedies** | Proportionate based on fault |
| **Pre-contract disclosure** | Only asked questions need answers |

## Policy Structure

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **Preamble** | Parties, insured, period, consideration |
| **Operative/insuring clauses** | Cover, subject matter, limits |
| **Exclusions** | What is not covered |
| **Conditions** | Precedent, continuing, warranty-like |
| **Definitions** | Term meanings |
| **Endorsements/clauses** | Modifications and market clauses |

## London Market Documents

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Slip** | Contract summary — terms, conditions, subscription lines |
| **Policy** | Formal evidence of cover |
| **Endorsements** | Changes to cover |
| **Wordings** | Full policy language |
| **Clauses** | Standard clauses (LMA, IUA, market) |
| **Bureau or coverholder documents** | Delegated authority agreements |

## LMA Standard Wordings

The **Lloyd's Market Association** produces standard clauses and wordings:

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **General exclusions** | Cyber, nuclear, sanctions |
| **Technical clauses** | Co-insurance, claims control |
| **Sector wordings** | Marine, aviation, energy, casualty |

Standard wordings bring **consistency and legal certainty**.

## Governing Law and Jurisdiction

| Element | Practice |
|---|---|
| **Governing law** | Usually English law |
| **Jurisdiction** | English courts, or arbitration (LMAA, ARIAS) |
| **Service of suit** | US-style clauses for NA risks |
| **Choice of law** | Respects party autonomy in commercial contracts |

## Contract Formation

```
Offer (proposal/client presentation) → Acceptance (quotation/binding) → Consideration (premium) → Policy issued
```

| Stage | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Proposal/submission** | Brokers present risk details |
| **Quotation** | Terms, conditions, premium |
| **Acceptance** | Binding or subject to policy |
| **Policy issue** | Formal contract document |

## Good Faith and Fair Presentation

- Business insureds must make a **fair presentation** of the risk.
- Failure can lead to **proportionate remedies** under the Insurance Act.
- Consumers answer **asked questions** with reasonable care (CIDRA).

## Utmost Good Faith

- **Utmost good faith (uberrimae fidei)** remains a principle.
- The Insurance Act 2015 reformulated the duty for business insurance as **fair presentation**.
- **Remedies** depend on the nature of non-disclosure or misrepresentation.

## Summary

- UK contracts rest on **English law, common law, and statute**.
- **Insurance Act 2015** reformed business insurance — fair presentation, remedies, warranties.
- **CIDRA** protects consumers with a reasonableness standard.
- The **London market** uses slips and LMA standard wordings.
- **Governing law and jurisdiction** clauses matter in international risks.
