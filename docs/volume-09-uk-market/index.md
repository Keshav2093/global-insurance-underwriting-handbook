# Volume 9 — The UK Insurance Market

Volume 9 covers the structure, regulation, and practice of the United Kingdom insurance market. It explains the **Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) and Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)** dual-regulatory framework, the Lloyd's market, distribution, and the conduct rules that govern UK insurers and intermediaries.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this volume you should be able to:

1. Describe the structure and size of the UK market.
2. Explain PRA/FCA regulation and Solvency II.
3. Understand UK policy structure and market forms.
4. Describe broker, MGA, and Lloyd's distribution.
5. Work within Lloyd's and subscription market practice.
