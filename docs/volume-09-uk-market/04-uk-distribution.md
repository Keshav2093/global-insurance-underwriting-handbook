# 9.4 Distribution & Intermediaries

## Overview

UK insurance is distributed through **brokers, agents, direct writers, and MGAs**. The London market relies heavily on **brokers** for access to capacity, and on **delegated underwriting authority** through coverholders. Intermediaries are **FCA-regulated** and subject to conduct, client money, and professional standards.

## Distribution Channels

| Channel | Description | Typical Lines |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct** | Online, telephone, digital | Personal lines |
| **Price comparison (aggregators)** | Compare and switch | Motor, home |
| **Broker** | Places coverage on behalf of insured | Commercial, specialty |
| **Agent** | Represents one or more insurers | Personal, SME |
| **Bancassurance** | Banks sell partner products | Life, home, travel |
| **MGA/coverholder** | Delegated underwriting authority | London market, specialty |
| **Corporate partnerships** | Affinity and employer programs | Health, protection |

## Brokers

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| **Retail broker** | Adviser to SME and corporate insureds |
| **Wholesale broker** | Places risks with Lloyd's and company markets |
| **London market broker** | Intermediary for subscription market access |
| **Reinsurance broker** | Places reinsurance for cedants |

Brokers are **FCA-authorized** and must meet conduct and capital requirements.

## The Broker's Role

```
Understand client need → Market the risk → Present to underwriters → Negotiate terms → Place and document → Ongoing service
```

| Stage | Activities |
|---|---|
| **Advice** | Assess exposure, coverage design |
| **Marketing** | Prepare submission, approach markets |
| **Placement** | Negotiate price and terms |
| **Documentation** | Policy, evidence, certificates |
| **Service** | Renewals, claims advocacy, risk advice |

## Agents and Tied Agents

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Independent agent** | Represents multiple principals |
| **Tied agent** | Acts for a single insurer under FCA rules |
| **Appointed representative** | Acts under an authorized principal's responsibility |

All are regulated through **FCA authorization or appointed representative (AR) status**.

## MGAs and Coverholders

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **MGA** | Delegated authority to underwrite, bind, and issue policies |
| **Coverholder** | Lloyd's-specific term for a delegated authority holder |
| **Binding authority agreement (BAA)** | The contract granting authority |
| **Underwriting guidelines** | Rules within which authority operates |

### BAA Controls

| Control | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Defined scope** | Lines, limits, territories |
| **Premium limits** | Binder capacity and authority |
| **Claims authority** | Settlement limits and referral |
| **Reporting** | Bordereaux and data reporting |
| **Audit** | Periodic review by the insurer |

## The Delegated Authority Process

```
Insurer grants BAA → Coverholder underwrites within guidelines → Bordereaux reported → Premiums remitted → Audits conducted
```

## Regulation of Intermediaries

| Requirement | Description |
|---|---|
| **FCA authorization** | Permission for regulated activities |
| **Conduct rules** | Honesty, integrity, skill, care |
| **Client money rules** | Separation and safeguarding |
| **Professional indemnity** | Minimum PI cover |
| **Financial crime** | AML/CFT procedures |
| **Product governance** | Target market and value |
| **Complaints handling** | DISP rules and FOS access |

## Remuneration and Conflicts

| Principle | Practice |
|---|---|
| **Transparency** | Disclosure of fees and commissions where required |
| **Fair value** | Remuneration must not impair good outcomes |
| **Conflict management** | Identify and manage conflicts of interest |
| **Inducements** | No inducements that breach conduct rules |

## The Future of Distribution

| Trend | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Digital platforms** | Direct and API-based distribution |
| **Embedded insurance** | Insurance in product journeys |
| **Open data** | Better comparison and personalization |
| **AI advice** | Automated guidance and suitability |
| **Consumer Duty** | Ongoing product and value governance |

## Summary

- **Brokers** dominate commercial and London market distribution.
- **MGAs and coverholders** operate under delegated authority with binders.
- Intermediaries are **FCA-regulated** with conduct, client money, and PI requirements.
- **Transparency and fair value** govern remuneration.
- **Digital and embedded models** are reshaping distribution.
