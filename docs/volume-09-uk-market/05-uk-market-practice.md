# 9.5 Underwriting & Market Practice

## Overview

The **London market** operates a distinctive underwriting model — risks are presented by **brokers**, underwritten on **slips**, and often **subscribed** by multiple insurers or syndicates. Practice is governed by market rules, delegated authority controls, and the **Lloyd's market framework**, plus the conduct of business standards under the FCA.

## The Underwriting Cycle in the London Market

```
Broker presents risk (submission) → Lead underwriter prices/terms → Following markets subscribe → Slip signed → Policy issued
```

## The Slip

The **slip** is the central London market document. It contains:

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| **Risk details** | Insured, subject, period, sums |
| **Coverage** | Perils, limits, deductibles |
| **Terms and conditions** | Exclusions, conditions, clauses |
| **Premium** | Rate, minimum deposit, adjustment terms |
| **Claims** | Notification and claims control clauses |
| **Subscription** | Lines and shares per insurer |

The slip is the **contract memorandum** until the policy is issued.

## The Subscription Market

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **Lead underwriter** | Sets terms and premium; first line |
| **Following underwriters** | Accept the risk on lead's terms |
| **Line** | Each subscriber's percentage share |
| **Signing** | Agreement evidenced on the slip |
| **Market security** | Lloyd's central fund; company market security |

## Roles in the Market

| Role | Function |
|---|---|
| **Broker** | Presents the risk; negotiates on the client's behalf |
| **Lead underwriter** | Prices and sets terms |
| **Following underwriter** | Subscribes on market terms |
| **Coverholder** | Binds business under a BAA |
| **Managing agent** | Operates a Lloyd's syndicate |
| **Lloyd's Corporation** | Market oversight and standards |

## Delegated Authority in Practice

| Element | Practice |
|---|---|
| **BAA** | Defines authority and limits |
| **Guidelines** | Underwriting rules and appetite |
| **Bordereaux** | Regular risk and premium data |
| **Premium trust** | Funds held and remitted |
| **Claims authority** | Settlement within limits; referrals above |
| **Audits** | Annual reviews of authority use |

## Market Standards

| Standard | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Lloyd's Minimum Standards** | Governance, underwriting, claims, conduct |
| **LMA clauses** | Standard terms and wordings |
| **Contract certainty** | Deadline to document contracts |
| **Claims agreements** | Property claims scheme for prompt settlement |
| **Financial crime** | Sanctions screening and AML |

## Pricing in the London Market

| Factor | Usage |
|---|---|
| **Rate on line (ROL)** | Premium as % of limit |
| **Basic premium** | Rate × limit/exposure |
| **Minimum and deposit premium** | Guarantee floor for adjustments |
| **Adjustment clauses** | Premium based on actual exposure (e.g., payroll, turnover) |
| **Catastrophe loading** | Exposure-based pricing |
| **Reinsurance cost** | Passing of reinsurance into premium |

## Underwriting Discipline

| Principle | Practice |
|---|---|
| **Risk selection** | Appetite and limits |
| **Exposure management** | Aggregation controls |
| **Data adequacy** | Complete and accurate submissions |
| **Documented decisions** | Anecdotes, declinatures, rationales |
| **Peer review** | Referral and sign-off processes |
| **Compliance** | Sanctions, conduct, licensing |

## Claims in the London Market

| Element | Practice |
|---|---|
| **Notification** | To lead and following underwriters |
| **Claims control clause** | Insurer rights to manage claims |
| **Claims agreement** | Advances and decisions |
| **Service providers** | Adjusters, lawyers, experts |
| **Subrogation** | Recovery actions |

## Current Market Practice Issues

| Issue | Consideration |
|---|---|
| **Contract certainty** | Policy issued on time |
| **Sanctions clauses** | LMA sanction limitation clauses |
| **Cyber coverage** | Exclusion and affirmative support |
| **Delegated authority** | Consistent governance of coverholders |
| **Digital placement** | PPL — Platform Placing Ltd; electronic slips |
| **ESG and climate** | Underwriting and investment standards |

## Summary

- The **slip** and **subscription model** define London market underwriting.
- The **lead underwriter** sets terms; **followers** subscribe.
- **Delegated authority** operates under BAA controls and audits.
- **Market standards** enforce contract certainty, claims, and conduct.
- **Pricing** uses ROL, minimums, deposits, and adjustment clauses.
