## Executive Summary

Treaty reinsurance covers a defined portfolio or class of business automatically within agreed terms. In a **quota share** treaty the ceding company and reinsurer share every risk in a fixed ratio. In a **surplus treaty** the reinsurer provides capacity above the cedent's retention, sharing the surplus line on a proportional basis. Both are proportional structures — premium and losses are shared in the same proportion as the risk.

---

## 15.3.1 Treaty Reinsurance Fundamentals

**Treaty reinsurance** is an agreement under which the reinsurer automatically accepts a defined share of all risks written by the ceding company falling within the treaty's scope, without individual risk submission.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Scope | Defined class / portfolio / territory |
| Obligation | Automatic — cedent must cede, reinsurer must accept |
| Term | Usually 12 months, renewable |
| Documentation | One treaty agreement |
| Premium | Ceded premium based on underlying business |
| Claims | Reinsurer pays its share per treaty terms |

### Types of Treaty

| Type | Category | Structure |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Quota share (QS) | Proportional | Fixed % of every risk |
| Surplus | Proportional | Capacity above retention, variable share |
| Excess of loss (XL) | Non-proportional | Losses above attachment |
| Stop loss | Non-proportional | Aggregate loss ratio protection |
| Catastrophe XL | Non-proportional | Single event protection |

---

## 15.3.2 Quota Share (QS) Treaty

**Quota share** reinsurance cedes a fixed percentage of every risk in the treaty portfolio to the reinsurer.

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Cession | Fixed % (e.g., 50%) of every risk |
| Retention | Remaining % retained by cedent |
| Premium | Same % of premium ceded |
| Losses | Same % of losses recovered |
| Purpose | Capital relief, portfolio sharing, profit sharing |
| Commission | Ceding commission often paid to cedent |

### Quota Share Illustration

| Risk | Sum Insured | QS Cession 40% | Cedent Retention 60% |
|------|-------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Risk A | $1,000,000 | $400,000 | $600,000 |
| Risk B | $2,500,000 | $1,000,000 | $1,500,000 |
| Risk C | $500,000 | $200,000 | $300,000 |

### Premium & Loss Sharing

| Item | Total | Cedent 60% | Reinsurer 40% |
|------|-------|------------|---------------|
| Premium | $100,000 | $60,000 | $40,000 |
| Loss | $50,000 | $30,000 | $20,000 |
| Commission | — | Received | Pays commission |

### Ceding Commission

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Purpose | Reimburse cedent's acquisition and administration costs |
| Calculation | % of ceded premium |
| Typical range | 20–35% depending on business |
| Profit commission | Additional share of reinsurer profit |

## 15.3.3 Surplus Treaty

**Surplus (surplus share) reinsurance** provides proportional coverage on individual risks above the ceding company's retention up to a defined number of lines.

| Concept | Definition |
|---------|------------|
| **Retention / net line** | Amount the cedent retains per risk |
| **Line** | Unit of retention (e.g., $500,000) |
| **Surplus capacity** | Number of lines (e.g., 10 lines = 10 × retention) |
| **Cession** | The surplus above retention ceded to reinsurer |
| **Automatic facility** | Cover provided automatically within capacity |

### Surplus Treaty Illustration

| Item | Risk A | Risk B |
|------|--------|--------|
| Sum insured | $500,000 | $3,000,000 |
| Retention (1 line) | $500,000 | $500,000 |
| Ceded to surplus | $0 | $2,500,000 |
| Lines used | 0 | 5 |
| Cedent % | 100% | 16.7% |
| Reinsurer % | 0% | 83.3% |

## 15.3.4 Quota Share vs Surplus Comparison

| Aspect | Quota Share | Surplus |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Share of risk | Fixed % always | Variable, above retention |
| Small risks | Ceded automatically | Fully retained (below retention) |
| Large risks | Ceded at same % | Ceded above retention |
| Purpose | Capital relief, portfolio sharing | Capacity, retention protection |
| Administration | Simple — fixed % | More complex — per-risk share |
| Adverse selection | Limited | Large risks ceded may be riskier |

### Key Calculation Formulas

| Formula | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| Cession % (surplus) | (Sum insured − Retention) ÷ Sum insured |
| Retention % | Retention ÷ Sum insured |
| Reinsurer share of loss | Loss × Cession % |
| Lines used | Cession ÷ Retention |

## 15.3.5 Treaty Administration

### Bordereau

| Content | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Premium bordereau | List of ceded risks with premiums |
| Loss bordereau | List of claims with recoveries |
| Frequency | Monthly or quarterly |
| Basis | Underlying policy data |

### Accounting

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Ceded premium** | Premium transferred to reinsurer |
| **Ceding commission** | Reinsurer pays cedent % of premium |
| **Profit commission** | Share of reinsurer's profit |
| **Recoveries** | Reinsurer's share of paid losses |
| **Reserves** | Funds withheld for outstanding claims |
| **Deposit premium** | Minimum premium at inception |

---

## 15.3.6 Underwriting Considerations for Proportional Treaties

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Retention adequacy** | Cedent retains meaningful risk |
| **Portfolio quality** | Mix of classes, territories, durations |
| **Ceding commission** | Appropriate for acquisition cost |
| **Claims management** | Cedent handles claims; follow-the-fortunes |
| **Reserving** | Adequate reserves for outstanding |
| **Accumulation** | Catastrophe exposure within proportional book |
| **Compliance** | Regulatory credit for reinsurance |

## 15.3.7 Worked Example — Surplus Treaty

> **Example:**
>
> - Retention: $1,000,000 (1 line)
> - Surplus capacity: 9 lines → $9,000,000
> - Risk: $5,000,000 sum insured
>
> **Calculation:**
>
> - Cession = $5,000,000 − $1,000,000 = $4,000,000
> - Lines used = 4
> - Cession % = 80%
> - Cedent share = 20%; loss of $500,000 → cedent pays $100,000, reinsurer pays $400,000

## Key Takeaways

1. **Quota share shares all risks in a fixed ratio.**
2. **Surplus treaties share the amount above a retention, up to capacity.**
3. **Premium, losses, and commissions follow the ceded share.**
4. **Proportional treaties require disciplined reporting (bordereaux).**
