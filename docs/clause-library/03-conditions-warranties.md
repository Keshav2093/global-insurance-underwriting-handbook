# Condition & Warranty Clauses

Conditions are requirements that must be met for coverage to apply. Warranties are promises that certain facts are, or will remain, true. Breach of a warranty can void coverage.

---

## 1. Types of Conditions

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Condition precedent** | Must be satisfied before coverage attaches | Protective safeguard warranty |
| **Condition subsequent** | Coverage may be lost if breached after inception | Notice of loss |
| **Promissory condition** | Promise that something will be done | Keep premises in good repair |
| **Affirmative warranty** | Statement of fact true at inception | Sprinkler system operational |
| **Promissory warranty** | Promise to maintain the state of affairs | Sprinkler system maintained |

---

## 2. Protective Safeguards Clause

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Effect** | Coverage is void if protective devices are not installed, maintained, or operational |
| **Examples** | Fire alarm, automatic sprinkler, central station monitoring |
| **Standard wording** | "As a condition of this insurance, the insured will keep the protective safeguards specified in the policy in working order." |
| **Application** | Applies at property locations listed |
| **Breach** | Void coverage (not merely suspend it) in many forms |

**Underwriting note:** Verify the protective devices exist, are monitored, and maintained. Confirm the insured understands this is a strict condition.

---

## 3. Vacancy / Unoccupancy Clause

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Vacant** | No contents and no people conducting normal operations |
| **Unoccupied** | No people but contents remain |
| **Standard property condition** | Coverage suspended for certain perils after the building has been vacant for 60+ days |
| **Suspended perils** | Vandalism, sprinkler leakage, building glass breakage, water damage, theft |
| **Fire, lightning, explosion** | Still covered but premium may be due |

### Vacancy Handling

| Situation | Recommended Action |
|-----------|--------------------|
| Planned vacancy under 60 days | Notify; consider exposure |
| Vacancy over 60 days | Place vacant building coverage (often at higher rate) |
| Permanent vacancy | Consider non-renewal or vacant property policy |
| Renovation vacancy | Monitor construction and security |

---

## 4. Maintenance Condition

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Required** | Keep the property and systems in good repair |
| **Purpose** | Prevent losses from deterioration |
| **Examples** | Roof repair, electrical maintenance, fire protection system testing |
| **Breach** | May void loss coverage caused by the neglect |

**Underwriting note:** Verified maintenance programs reduce loss frequency. Document any known deferred maintenance.

---

## 5. Material Change Clause

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Requirement** | Insured must notify the insurer of material changes in the risk |
| **Examples** | New operations, vacancy, construction, ownership change, increased values |
| **Effect of notice** | Insurer can adjust premium or terms |
| **Failure to notify** | May result in non-coverage that is triggered by the undisclosed change |

**Underwriting note:** Emphasize the insured's obligation to report changes mid-term. Underwrite changes promptly to preserve coverage integrity.

---

## 6. Supervision & Control Condition

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Requirement** | Operations are supervised by competent personnel |
| **Use** | Common in liability coverages for licensed trades |
| **Purpose** | Ensures safe operations and skill standards |
| **Breach** | May void coverage where the risk arises from unsupervised work |

---

## 7. Notice of Claim Condition

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Requirement** | Prompt notice of a loss or claim |
| **Standard wording** | "In the event of a claim the insured shall give notice as soon as practicable." |
| **Purpose** | Allows investigation, mitigation, defense |
| **Late notice** | May limit or void coverage if the insurer is prejudiced |

---

## 8. Rights of Subrogation

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Definition** | Insurer steps into the insured's rights to recover from a third party |
| **Waiver** | Often waived in contracts by mutual agreement |
| **Effect of waiver** | May increase risk — consider when the insured waives subrogation |

---

## 9. Examination of Books & Records

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Requirement** | Insurer may examine the insured's books and records |
| **Purpose** | Verify premium basis (payroll, sales) and claims |
| **Applies to** | Audit-able exposures (workers comp, liability based on payroll) |

---

## 10. Other Common Conditions

| Clause | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cancellation** | Both parties may cancel with notice (varies by jurisdiction) |
| **Declarations** | Basis of the contract; misrepresentation voids coverage |
| **Transfer of interest** | Policy not assignable without consent |
| **No benefit to bailee** | Bailee cannot benefit from the policy |
| **Suit against us** | Insured can only sue after complying with policy conditions |
| **Limitation of action** | Legal action must begin within the state statute of limitations |
| **Two or more coverages** | How limits apply when multiple coverages respond |

---

## 11. Warranties in Underwriting

| Warranty Type | Example | Consequence of Breach |
|---------------|---------|-----------------------|
| **Affirmative** | All employees 18+ years of age | Void coverage |
| **Promissory** | Maintain a watchman 24/7 | Void coverage while breached |
| **Statement of fact** | Building sprinklered | Basis of contract |

### Underwriting Best Practices

| Practice | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Disclosure** | Explain warranties clearly to the insured |
| **Feasibility** | Confirm the insured can comply |
| **Verification** | Verify at survey / renewal |
| **Documentation** | Record warranties in the file |
| **Enforcement** | Apply consistently |
