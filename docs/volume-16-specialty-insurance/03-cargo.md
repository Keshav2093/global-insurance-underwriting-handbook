## Executive Summary

Cargo insurance protects goods in transit by sea, air, road, and rail. Cover may be on open cover or voyage basis with institute cargo clauses.

---

## 16.3.1 Cargo — Overview

Marine Cargo is a specialised class of insurance requiring technical underwriting knowledge and tailored policy wordings.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Market | Specialist insurers, Lloyd's, brokers |
| Underwriting | Technical, case-by-case |
| Coverage | Cargo risk-specific wordings |
| Rating | Exposure and experience-based |

---

## 16.3.2 Coverages

| Coverage | Description | Typical Limit |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| All risks | Broad transit cover | Consignment value |
| Named perils | Specific risks | Consignment value |
| War & strikes | Political perils | Additional |

---

## 16.3.3 Key Risks

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Commodity | Hazard and packing | Condition surveys |
| Transit route | Exposure to theft, damage | Routing controls |
| Carrier | Reliability and security | Approved carriers |

---

## 16.3.4 Underwriting Information

| Information | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| Commodity | Nature and packing |
| Transit | Route and modes |
| Values | Consignment values |

## 16.3.5 Rating Factors

| Factor | Impact | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|

## 16.3.6 Exclusions & Conditions

| Exclusion / Condition | Reason |
|-----------------------|--------|
