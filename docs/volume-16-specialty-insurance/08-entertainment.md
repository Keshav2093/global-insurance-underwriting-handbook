## Executive Summary

Entertainment insurance covers film, television, events, performances, casts, and equipment. Production and cancellation risks are central.

---

## 16.8.1 Entertainment — Overview

Entertainment Insurance is a specialised class of insurance requiring technical underwriting knowledge and tailored policy wordings.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Market | Specialist insurers, Lloyd's, brokers |
| Underwriting | Technical, case-by-case |
| Coverage | Entertainment risk-specific wordings |
| Rating | Exposure and experience-based |

---

## 16.8.2 Coverages

| Coverage | Description | Typical Limit |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| Production | Cast, props, negatives | Budget-based |
| Cancellation | Event cancellation | Event budget |
| Equipment | Cameras, sound, gear | Schedule |

---

## 16.8.3 Key Risks

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Production type | Film, TV, live event | Schedule control |
| Cast risk | Key personnel | Key person protection |
| Location | Schedule and geography | Location plans |

---

## 16.8.4 Underwriting Information

| Information | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| Budget | Scale of production |
| Cast | Key personnel |
| Schedule | Timeline |

---

## 16.8.5 Rating Factors

| Factor | Impact | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Budget | High | Premium on budget |
| Cast | Medium | Key person risk |
| Schedule | Medium | Exposure duration |

---

## 16.8.6 Exclusions & Conditions

| Exclusion / Condition | Reason |
|-----------------------|--------|
| Nuclear | Standing exclusion |
| War & terrorism | Separate cover |

## 16.8.7 Claims & Risk Engineering

| Stage | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Notification | Prompt notice, specialist adjusters |
| Investigation | Technical experts, causation analysis |
| Settlement | Within limits, subrogation reviewed |

## 16.8.8 Reinsurance & Capacity

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Capacity | Large limits via facultative/treaty |
| Accumulation | Geographic and modal exposures |
| Security | Reinsurer ratings and collateral |
