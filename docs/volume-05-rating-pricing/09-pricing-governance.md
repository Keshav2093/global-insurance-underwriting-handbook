# 5.9 Pricing Governance & Compliance

## Overview

Pricing is regulated and must be **documented, fair, and sustainable**. Governance ensures that rates are adequate, not unfairly discriminatory, and comply with law. It also protects the insurer from pricing errors that undermine solvency or reputation.

## Why Pricing Governance Matters

| Risk | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Inadequate rates** | Losses exceed premium; insolvency |
| **Unfair discrimination** | Regulatory action, litigation, reputational harm |
| **Rate filing non-compliance** | Fines, forced refunds, license issues |
| **Model error** | Inaccurate classes and prices |
| **Undocumented judgment** | Inconsistent decisions, audit failures |

## Rate Regulation — US Model

| Concept | Description |
|---|---|
| **Prior approval** | Rates must be filed and approved before use |
| **File and use** | Filed and effective immediately; regulator can object |
| **Use and file** | Used, then filed |
| **Open competition** | Minimal filing; market disciplines rates |

All US states require rates that are **not excessive, not inadequate, and not unfairly discriminatory**.

## Rate Filing Content

A filing demonstrates:

- **Data** — premium, exposure, loss experience.
- **Method** — loss cost or loss ratio method.
- **Adjustments** — development, trend, credibility.
- **Relativities** — class, territory, limit, deductible.
- **Expense provisions** — acquisition, general, profit.
- **Support** — actuarial memoranda, exhibits, validation.

## Fairness and Discrimination

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Unfair discrimination** | Same risk charged different rates without actuarial justification |
| **Fair discrimination** | Different rates justified by expected loss differences |
| **Prohibited factors** | Race, religion, national origin, gender (where barred), genetic info |

Pricing must avoid **direct and indirect** use of prohibited factors (e.g., proxies in models).

## Adverse Action and Consumer Notices

- **Adverse action** — denial, non-renewal, higher premium based on consumer report triggers notice requirements.
- **FCRA (US)** — adverse action notice; right to dispute.
- **State laws** — notices for credit-based scores, MVR use.
- **UK FCA** — fair value assessments; no "loyalty penalty" for renewals.

## UK Regulatory Approach

| Requirement | Description |
|---|---|
| **Fair value** | Product and price must deliver fair value (Consumer Duty) |
| **Risk-based pricing** | Prices reflect risk; no unjustified cross-subsidy |
| **Pricing practices** | Renewal pricing must not penalize loyalty |
| **Data protection** | GDPR — lawful basis, minimization |
| **Model governance** | AI Act — high-risk systems require oversight |

## Pricing Monitoring

| Metric | Use |
|---|---|
| **Actual vs. indicated rate** | Discipline of underwriters |
| **Rate realization** | Average premium vs. manual |
| **Loss ratio by class** | Relative profitability |
| **Retention by segment** | Customer behavior |
| **Price monitoring** | Adherence to guidelines |
| **Model drift** | Performance over time |
| **Complaint rates** | Consumer harms |

## Documentation Standards

A pricing file should support **every decision**:

| Element | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Memo** | Basis of the indicated rate |
| **Data** | Source, period, definitions |
| **Assumptions** | Trend, expenses, credibility |
| **Judgment** | Where discretion was used |
| **Approval** | Sign-off by actuarial/management |
| **Retention** | Maintained per policy |

## Pricing Committees and Controls

- **Pricing committee** — approves rate changes, discount programs, exceptions.
- **Underwriting authority** — sets limits on discretionary deviation.
- **Independent validation** — actuaries validate models and ratings.
- **Audit** — internal and external audit of pricing processes.
- **Regulatory reporting** — rates ROR (rates, rules, and forms) documentation.

## Cycle Discipline and Market Conduct

| Theme | Practice |
|---|---|
| **Soft market** | Do not chase volume below indicated rates |
| **Fair competition** | Price on merits, not predatory behavior |
| **Transparency** | Clear explanations of pricing to customers |
| **Sustainability** | Rates must be self-supporting over time |

## Summary

- Pricing must be **adequate, not unfairly discriminatory, and lawful**.
- **Rate filings** document data, methods, and assumptions.
- **Adverse action rules** protect consumers using third-party data.
- **Fair value (UK) and anti-discrimination (US)** govern the same goal: fair pricing.
- **Monitoring and documentation** maintain discipline and auditability.
- **Model governance** (AI Act, FCRA) constrains automated pricing.
