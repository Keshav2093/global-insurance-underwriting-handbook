# Volume 5 — Rating & Pricing

Volume 5 covers how insurance premiums are calculated: from the fundamentals of pricing to the data, techniques, and models used across lines. It explains the difference between rating (applying approved rates) and pricing (setting the level of rates), and how actuaries and underwriters work together.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this volume you should be able to:

1. Explain the components of premium and the pricing equation.
2. Understand loss reserving, triangles, and IBNR.
3. Describe classification and rating factors across lines.
4. Apply the loss cost and loss ratio pricing methods.
5. Describe predictive modeling in pricing and its governance.
6. Rate the principal property/casualty lines.
7. Explain experience, schedule, and retrospective rating.
8. Describe reinsurance structures and pricing.
9. Explain pricing governance: filings, fairness, and monitoring.
