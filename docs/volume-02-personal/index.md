# Volume 2 — Personal Insurance

Volume 2 covers insurance for individuals and families. It explains the personal lines products — auto, homeowners, dwelling fire, personal umbrella, and life and health essentials — from the perspective of an underwriter: what is covered, what drives risk, and what underwriting and rating considerations apply.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this volume you should be able to:

1. Describe the personal lines marketplace and how products are sold.
2. Explain the coverages, exclusions, and rating factors of the Personal Auto Policy.
3. Explain the homeowner forms and their property/liability protections.
4. Identify the underwriting considerations for dwelling fire, condo, tenant, and mobile home risks.
5. Explain how personal umbrella coverage interacts with underlying policies.
6. Distinguish life insurance products and the key underwriting factors.
7. Describe health, disability, and accident product essentials.
8. Use account-level (package) underwriting across personal lines.
