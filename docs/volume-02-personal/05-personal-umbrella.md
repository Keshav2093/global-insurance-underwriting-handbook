# 2.5 Personal Umbrella & Excess Liability

## Overview

A **personal umbrella policy (PUP)** provides excess liability protection above the limits of the insured's underlying policies (home, auto, watercraft) and extends coverage to some gaps not covered by the underlying policies. It is designed for individuals with assets to protect or a high risk of liability judgment.

- **Excess coverage** — pays when the underlying policy limits are exhausted.
- **Drop-down coverage** — extends coverage to claims not covered by an underlying policy (subject to a self-insured retention, SIR).
- **Broad coverage** — may include libel, slander, defamation, false arrest, and other personal injury offenses.

Umbrella liability is typically written in increments:

- \$1,000,000
- \$2,000,000
- \$5,000,000
- \$10,000,000 (higher limits written on a case-by-case basis)

## Coverage Structure

### Excess Over Underlying

The umbrella sits above:

| Underlying Policy | Typical Required Minimum Limit |
|---|---|
| Auto liability | \$250,000–\$500,000 per person / \$500,000–\$1,000,000 per occurrence (state-dependent) |
| Homeowners liability | \$300,000–\$500,000 |
| Watercraft liability | \$300,000–\$1,000,000 (if owned) |
| Other liability | As required (e.g., recreational vehicles, rental properties) |

The umbrella responds when the underlying limit is exhausted, subject to the terms of the umbrella.

### Drop-Down / SIR

- For claims not covered by an underlying policy, the umbrella may provide coverage subject to a **self-insured retention** (typically \$250–\$2,500).
- The insured is responsible for the SIR before the umbrella responds.
- Not all umbrella policies provide drop-down; read the form carefully.

### Common Exclusions

- Intentional acts.
- Business and professional activities (unless endorsed).
- Aircraft.
- Workers compensation / employers liability.
- Punitive damages (many states).
- Certain watercraft unless scheduled.
- Damage to the insured's own property.
- Contractual liability assumed by the insured (subject to terms).

## Underwriting Personal Umbrella

The primary underwriting question: **is the risk acceptable and is the underlying coverage verified?**

### Requirements

1. **Underlying policies must be in force** at the required limits.
2. **Underlying insurers must be acceptable** (financially sound; admitted).
3. **All household drivers and vehicles** must be listed and rated.
4. **Watercraft and recreational vehicles** must be declared and underwritten separately if above a size threshold.
5. **The insured's assets and exposures** must be consistent with the limit requested.

### Underwriting Data Sources

- Application (assets, vehicles, watercraft, rental properties, pets).
- Underlying policy declarations pages.
- MVR / CLUE for the household.
- Inspection if complex exposures (high-value home, large watercraft).

### Decline Triggers

- Unacceptable underlying carriers or lapsed coverages.
- High-risk drivers (DUI, multiple violations/accidents) not acceptable.
- Unscheduled watercraft or RVs.
- Business exposures not covered.
- Intentional or criminal activity exposures.
- Uninsurable pets (certain breeds) if liability history exists.

## Rating Personal Umbrella

Umbrella premiums are relatively low relative to limit because severity is low-frequency. Pricing factors:

- Number of cars and drivers.
- Number of homes and other real estate.
- Watercraft.
- Underlying limits carried.
- Territory.
- Loss history of the household.
- Limit selected.

Example (illustrative): base \$250 for \$1M; add \$50 per additional vehicle; add \$100 for second home; add \$150–\$300 for a watercraft; credits for higher underlying limits.

## UK Perspective

- **Personal liability / "umbrella" products** are less standardized in the UK; excess layer products exist but are less common.
- UK personal liability is usually embedded in **home contents policies** (liability section) with modest limits (e.g., £2M–£5M legal liability is common under home emergency / legal protection or as "personal liability" cover).
- **Individual "legal liability" policies** and **personal injury cover** may supplement.
- Where high limits are required (e.g., public figures), bespoke products or excess liability through brokers are arranged.

## Claims Considerations

- **Trigger** — underlying limit exhaustion or covered drop-down claim.
- **Notice** — prompt notice to the umbrella carrier of any claim that may involve the umbrella.
- **Defense** — the umbrella may provide defense when underlying is exhausted or the claim is outside underlying.
- **Allocation** — defense costs and indemnity may be allocated between underlying layers.

## Summary

- The personal umbrella gives **excess limits and broadened drop-down cover** for a relatively low premium.
- Underwriting centers on **verifying underlying coverages, household exposures, and risk acceptability**.
- Careful **declaration of vehicles, homes, watercraft, and business use** is essential.
