### E-Mod Assessment

| E-Mod | Assessment |
|-------|-----------|
| Below 0.90 | Favorable — strong experience |
| 0.90–1.10 | Average |
| 1.11–1.30 | Above average — review causes |
| Above 1.30 | Poor — underwriting conditions likely |
