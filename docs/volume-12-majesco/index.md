# Volume 12 — Majesco

Volume 12 covers Majesco — a leading provider of P&C and L&A insurance core systems and digital platform solutions. It explains the Majesco platform, product suite, architecture, and implementation approach.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this volume you should be able to:

1. Explain the Majesco platform and its product suite.
2. Describe Majesco's cloud-native architecture.
3. Describe Majesco Policy for P&C policy administration.
4. Describe Majesco Billing and Claims capabilities.
5. Use Majesco data and analytics tools.
6. Plan a Majesco implementation.
7. Compare Majesco, Guidewire, and Duck Creek.
