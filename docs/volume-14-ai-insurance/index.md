# Volume 14 — AI in Insurance

Volume 14 covers artificial intelligence and machine learning in the insurance industry: how AI is used across underwriting, claims, pricing, distribution, and operations; the data and model lifecycle; governance and ethics; and practical implementation.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this volume you should be able to:

1. Explain AI use cases across the insurance value chain.
2. Describe the data foundations needed for AI.
3. Explain the model development lifecycle.
4. Describe AI applications in underwriting.
5. Describe AI applications in claims.
6. Apply AI governance principles — fairness, explainability, compliance.
