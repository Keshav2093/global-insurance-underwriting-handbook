# Volume 7 — Claims

Volume 7 covers the claims function — from first notice of loss through investigation, coverage analysis, resolution, and recovery. It explains the claims process, the skills and tools used by adjusters, and the legal and regulatory framework that governs fair, prompt, and accurate claim handling.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this volume you should be able to:

1. Explain the role of claims in the insurance operation.
2. Investigate a claim from notification to conclusion.
3. Analyze coverage and apply policy interpretation.
4. Resolve claims fairly and efficiently.
5. Set reserves and manage settlement authority.
6. Handle recoveries: reinsurance, subrogation, salvage.
7. Use claims systems, data, and analytics.
8. Apply compliance and best-practice standards.
