# 7.2 Notification & Investigation

## Overview

The claim begins with **notification** and proceeds through **investigation** — gathering the facts needed to determine coverage, liability, and value. Thorough, timely investigation is the foundation of fair and accurate claims handling.

## First Notice of Loss (FNOL)

| Element | Details Captured |
|---|---|
| **Insured** | Policy number, contact, relationship |
| **Loss details** | Date, time, location, description |
| **Damage/injury** | Nature, extent, value indicators |
| **Third parties** | Names, contact, witnesses |
| **Police/regulatory** | Report numbers, citations |
| **Prior claims** | History and context |

FNOL must be **prompt** — regulatory deadlines may apply to acknowledgment and contact.

## Notification Channels

- Phone, portal, mobile app, email, agent/broker.
- **24/7 intake** for auto and property.
- **Catastrophe surge** — mass notification response plans.

## Verification Steps

1. **Policy in force** — effective dates, status.
2. **Coverage applicable** — the loss event and coverage trigger.
3. **Notice compliance** — the insured's duty to give prompt notice.
4. **Premium paid** — payment status where relevant.
5. **Potential exclusions** — early identifiers.

## The Investigation Plan

| Scope | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Liability** | Fault, comparative negligence, contracts |
| **Coverage** | Occurrence, exclusions, conditions |
| **Damages** | Property, injury, BI, defense costs |
| **Subrogation** | Third-party fault, contractual indemnity |
| **Fraud indicators** | SIU criteria, red flags |

## Investigation Activities

| Activity | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Take statement** | Insured, witnesses, claimant |
| **Inspect scene/property** | Damage assessment, photos |
| **Review records** | Police, medical, employment, financial |
| **Interview experts** | Engineers, medical, forensic |
| **Obtain documents** | Contracts, invoices, payroll |
| **Check databases** | Loss history, fraud indicators |
| **Verify facts** | Coverage, limits, condition |

## Recorded statements and Examinations

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| **Signed statement** | Claimant version of facts |
| **Recorded statement** | Consistent record; legal limits apply |
| **EUO (Examination Under Oath)** | Insured's duty to cooperate; permitted per policy |
| **IME (Independent Medical Exam)** | Workers comp / disability verification |

Each tool has **legal and regulatory boundaries** — use within the policy and law.

## Liability Analysis

- **Third-party claims** — determine the insured's legal liability.
- **Comparative/contributory negligence** — allocation.
- **Contracts** — indemnity and additional insured status.
- **Statutory duties** — premises liability, product defect, auto fault.

## Reports and Documentation

| Document | Content |
|---|---|
| **Preliminary report** | Facts, coverage, initial assessment |
| **Investigation file** | Statements, photos, records, expert reports |
| **Coverage memo** | Analysis of applicability and reservations |
| **Reserve recommendation** | Initial and updated case estimates |

Documentation must be **objective, complete, and reviewable**.

## Timeliness and Communication

| Requirement | Practice |
|---|---|
| **Acknowledge FNOL** | Within regulatory deadlines |
| **Contact insured** | Promptly; explain next steps |
| **Update claimant** | Periodic status communications |
| **Scope authority** | Advise of investigation or coverage issues |
| **Meet deadlines** | Statutory and internal SLAs |

## Summary

- **Notification** captures the claim and verifies policy applicability.
- **Investigation** gathers facts: statements, records, inspections, experts.
- **Liability and coverage analysis** drive the decision.
- **Documentation** must be complete and objective.
- Prompt, transparent communication is both regulatory and good practice.
