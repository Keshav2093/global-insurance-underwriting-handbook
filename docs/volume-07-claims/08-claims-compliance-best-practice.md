# 7.8 Claims Compliance & Best Practice

## Overview

Claims are one of the most **regulated and scrutinized** functions in insurance. Fair, prompt, and accurate claim handling is both a legal requirement and the foundation of trust. Compliance failures produce regulatory action, litigation, and reputational damage.

## Fair Claims Practices

| Principle | Application |
|---|---|
| **Prompt acknowledgment** | Respond to claims within deadlines |
| **Complete investigation** | Reasonable inquiry before decision |
| **Fair settlement** | Offer what is owed; no lowballing |
| **No unreasonable delay** | Timely decisions and payments |
| **Clear communication** | Explain decisions and policy basis |
| **No misrepresentation** | Never misstate policy terms or coverage |
| **Good faith** | Act honestly toward the insured |

Most jurisdictions have **Unfair Claims Settlement Practices Acts** (or equivalent) with specific prohibited acts.

## Regulatory Frameworks

| Framework | Scope |
|---|---|
| **US state regulations** | Claims practice acts, unfair trade practices, licensing |
| **NAIC model acts** | Model unfair claims settlement practices |
| **UK FCA rules** | Fair treatment of customers, claims outcomes |
| **EU/EEA** | Conduct of business, consumer protection |
| **Market conduct exams** | State reviews of claims practices |

See [1.5 The Legal Environment](../volume-01-fundamentals/05-legal-environment.md).

## Compliance Requirements

| Requirement | Practice |
|---|---|
| **Acknowledgment of notice** | Within regulatory timeframe |
| **Coverage decision** | Within statutory deadline |
| **Claim payment** | Promptly after agreement or judgment |
| **Denial explanation** | Written, with policy basis |
| **Reservation of rights** | Issued when coverage uncertain |
| **Record retention** | Claims files kept per schedule |
| **Complaint handling** | Centralized, timely, reported |
| **Privacy** | Protect claimant and medical data |

## Best-Practice Framework

```
Acknowledge → Investigate → Determine → Communicate → Resolve → Document
```

| Step | Best Practice |
|---|---|
| **Acknowledge** | Immediate, with next steps |
| **Investigate** | Thorough, documented, objective |
| **Determine** | Coverage analysis, reserves, authority |
| **Communicate** | Regular, clear, honest updates |
| **Resolve** | Fair settlement; prompt payment |
| **Document** | Complete file; audit-ready |

## Documentation Standards

| Element | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Chronology** | Complete timeline of the claim |
| **Coverage memo** | Policy analysis and decision |
| **Investigation records** | Statements, photos, reports |
| **Authority approvals** | Settlement and payment authorization |
| **Communications** | Insured, claimant, counsel |
| **Reserve history** | Estimates and changes |
| **Payments** | Amounts, payees, releases |

A well-documented file **proves good faith**.

## Quality Assurance

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| **File reviews** | Periodic audits of claim files |
| **Coverage audits** | Verify interpretation consistency |
| **Reserve reviews** | Adequacy and reasonableness |
| **Cycle time monitoring** | Timeliness compliance |
| **Customer feedback** | Complaint trends, NPS |
| **Compliance reporting** | Regulatory and internal metrics |

## The Insured's Experience

| Expectation | Practice |
|---|---|
| **Being heard** | Active listening; respect |
| **Understanding** | Plain-language explanations |
| **Certainty** | Timely decisions, no mixed messages |
| **Control** | Insured is kept informed at each step |
| **Fairness** | Outcome matches the facts and policy |

## Emerging Issues

| Issue | Consideration |
|---|---|
| **AI in claims** | Fairness, transparency, human review |
| **Cyber claims** | Data breach, notification, privacy |
| **Climate events** | Category events, surge handling, coverage disputes |
| **Litigation trends** | Social inflation, nuclear verdicts |
| **Regulatory change** | Consumer duty, ESG reporting |

## Summary

- Claims must be **fair, prompt, accurate, and transparent**.
- **Statutes and regulations** define prohibited practices.
- **Documentation** proves good faith; gaps create risk.
- **Quality assurance** monitors compliance and outcomes.
- The **customer experience** is the measure of claims quality.
