# Volume 6 — Policy Administration

Volume 6 covers the policy administration function — from the initial policy lifecycle through issuance, endorsements, renewals, cancellations, and servicing. It explains how policies are created, maintained, and terminated, and the systems, premium handling, and compliance standards that keep the process accurate and lawful.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this volume you should be able to:

1. Explain the policy lifecycle from quote to renewal.
2. Issue accurate policy documents with proper evidence.
3. Process endorsements and policy changes correctly.
4. Manage renewals and statutory notices.
5. Handle cancellations and non-renewals in compliance.
6. Manage premium billing, collection, and audits.
7. Use policy administration systems and data.
8. Apply servicing and compliance standards.
