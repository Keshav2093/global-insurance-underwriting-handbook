# 6.1 Policy Lifecycle

## Overview

Every insurance policy follows a **lifecycle** — from quotation and issuance through mid-term changes, renewal, and final expiry or cancellation. The policy administration function ensures each stage is processed accurately and on time.

## The Policy Lifecycle

```
Quote → Issue → In-force → Mid-term change → Renewal / Expiry / Cancellation
```

| Stage | Key Activities |
|---|---|
| **Quotation** | Data capture, rating, terms offered |
| **Issuance** | Policy documents issued; premium collected; coverage begins |
| **In-force** | Endorsements, billing, claims, servicing |
| **Mid-term change** | Adjust limits, values, drivers, premises; premium correction |
| **Renewal** | Re-underwrite, re-rate, issuance of renewal documents |
| **Expiry** | Policy ends; claims reporting period |
| **Cancellation** | Mid-term termination; refund or additional premium |

## Quotation to Issuance

| Step | Description |
|---|---|
| 1. **Submission** | Application data from agent/broker or customer |
| 2. **Rating** | Apply rate table or model; determine premium |
| 3. **Underwriting** | Eligibility, referral, terms |
| 4. **Bind** | Agreement to provide coverage as of a date |
| 5. **Issue** | Generate policy documents and schedule |
| 6. **Collect** | Premium payment or installment plan |
| 7. **Deliver** | Send policy to insured/producer |
| 8. **Verify** | Confirm data and documents correct |

## Key Policy Data

| Data Element | Example |
|---|---|
| **Named insured** | Legal entity and address |
| **Policy period** | Effective and expiry dates |
| **Coverages & limits** | Per coverage and policy limits |
| **Exposures** | Vehicles, locations, payroll, values |
| **Deductibles** | Per coverage |
| **Premium** | Base, taxes, fees, payment plan |
| **Forms & endorsements** | Applicable policy forms |
| **Insurer & producer** | Underwriting company, agency |

## The In-Force Period

During the term, policy administration supports:

- **Endorsements** — mid-term changes.
- **Billing** — installments, renewals, adjustments.
- **Claims** — notification and handling (Volume 7).
- **Audits** — premium audits for exposure-based policies.
- **Compliance** — cancels, notices, regulatory filings.
- **Customer service** — questions, certificates, evidence.

## Renewal

- **Renewal notice** — sent before expiry (typically 30–60 days).
- **Re-underwriting** — review current data, claims, and risk changes.
- **Re-rating** — new rate level, exposure base, terms.
- **Declination** — notice of non-renewal with reasons where required.
- **Retention** — ensure continuous coverage and no gaps.

See [6.4 Renewals](04-renewals.md).

## Cancellation and Expiry

| Event | Description |
|---|---|
| **Expiry** | Term ends naturally; coverage ceases; report period for claims |
| **Cancellation by insured** | Mid-term cancellation; short-rate refund |
| **Cancellation by insurer** | Non-payment, material misrepresentation, fraud, risk deterioration |
| **Non-renewal** | Insurer declines to renew at expiry |
| **Rescission/void** | Coverage void ab initio (fraud) |

Refunds and additional premiums are calculated per the **cancellation provisions** of the policy and **state law**.

See [6.5 Cancellations & Non-renewals](05-cancellations-nonrenewals.md).

## Lifecycle Control Points

| Control | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Effective date validation** | Proper binding and retroactivity |
| **Document generation** | Issued vs. pending tracking |
| **Premium verification** | Collect or bill before bind where required |
| **Audit trail** | Every change logged with user, date, reason |
| **Reconciliation** | Premiums, endorsements, and documents match |
| **Compliance checks** | Notices, cancellations, state rules |

## Common Lifecycle Errors

| Error | Impact |
|---|---|
| Wrong effective date | Coverage gap or retroactive exposure |
| Incorrect exposure basis | Wrong premium and potential coinsurance |
| Missing endorsement | Coverage mismatch at claim |
| Delayed renewal | Lapse and reinstatement issues |
| Improper cancellation notice | Regulatory violation and coverage challenge |
| Unreconciled premium | Accounting and audit issues |

## Summary

- The lifecycle is **quote → issue → in-force → change → renewal/cancellation**.
- **Issuance, endorsements, renewal, and cancellation** each have strict data and timing requirements.
- **Controls** (effective dates, documents, premiums, audit trail) protect accuracy.
- **Errors** at any stage produce coverage, premium, or compliance problems.
