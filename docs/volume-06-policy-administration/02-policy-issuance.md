# 6.2 Policy Issuance & Documentation

## Overview

Policy issuance converts a bound submission into a **valid, documented insurance contract**. The policy documents define exactly what is covered, for whom, for what period, at what premium, and subject to what conditions.

## The Policy Document Set

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Declarations page (Dec page)** | Summary of insured, period, coverages, limits, premium |
| **Policy form / wording** | The full contractual terms and conditions |
| **Schedules** | Lists of locations, vehicles, items, values |
| **Forms & endorsements** | Amendments to the base form |
| **Certificates of insurance** | Evidence of coverage for third parties |
| **Insuring agreement** | Statement of what is covered |
| **Conditions** | Duties, cancellation, claims provisions |
| **Exclusions** | What is not covered |

## The Declarations Page

The Dec page is the **summary of the contract**:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Policy number | CP-1001234 |
| Named insured | ABC Manufacturing Inc. |
| Policy period | 01/01/2026 – 01/01/2027 |
| Coverages | Building, Contents, BI, Liability |
| Limits | $2,000,000 / $500,000 / $250,000 |
| Deductibles | $10,000 property |
| Premium | $18,500 plus taxes/fees |
| Forms | CP 00 10, CP 10 30, etc. |
| Insurer/agency | Company, agency, contact |

## Policy Form / Wording

The form contains:

- **Insuring agreement** — the promise to pay.
- **Definitions** — key terms and their meaning.
- **Exclusions** — what is not covered.
- **Conditions** — duties, cancellation, suit, subrogation.
- **Deductibles and coinsurance** — the insured's retention.
- **Limits of liability** — per occurrence, aggregate, per person.

## Issuance Process

```
1. Bind coverage (authority + data complete)
2. Verify underwriting conditions met (inspection, payment)
3. Generate documents (system or manual)
4. Quality check (data, forms, premium)
5. Deliver to insured/producer
6. Record issuance (policy file, system, documents)
7. Confirm premium collection/billing
```

## Issuance Timing and Effective Dates

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Standard new business** | Coverage effective at 12:01 AM local time of effective date |
| **Backdated binders** | Allowed only with authority; date on binder |
| **Retroactive cover** | Requires specific underwriting approval |
| **Evidence of insurance** | Certificate issued; does not amend policy terms |

## Delivery and Evidence

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Paper** | Mailed or hand-delivered; proof of delivery retained |
| **Electronic** | Email or portal; consent for e-delivery may be required |
| **Certificate** | Not a policy; evidence only |
| **Binder** | Temporary contract pending policy issue |

## Quality Checks

| Check | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Named insured** | Correct legal entity and spelling |
| **Date** | Effective/expiry, no gaps |
| **Limits** | Match application and underwriting |
| **Exposures** | Correct premises, vehicles, values |
| **Premium** | Rate × exposure = charged premium |
| **Forms** | Correct editions and endorsements |
| **Taxes/fees** | Proper statutory charges |

## Issuance Errors and Corrections

| Error Type | Correction |
|---|---|
| **Clerical** | Corrective endorsement; evidence of correction |
| **Underwriting miss** | Endorse to correct terms; notify insured |
| **Premium error** | Additional or return premium |
| **Coverage gap** | Endorse retroactively if covered under binder authority |
| **Wrong form** | Replace form from inception if within authority |

## Recordkeeping

- Policy file retains the **complete document set**.
- **Audit trail** — every version, change, and activity logged.
- **Retention** per regulatory and corporate schedule.
- **Indexing** — searchable by policy number, insured, date.

## Summary

- Issuance produces the **declarations, form, schedules, and endorsements**.
- The **Dec page** is the summary; the **form** is the contract.
- **Quality checks** verify data, dates, limits, exposures, premium, and forms.
- **Delivery, records, and audit trail** support compliance.
- Errors must be **corrected and documented** promptly.
