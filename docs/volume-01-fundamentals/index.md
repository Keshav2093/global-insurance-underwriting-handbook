# Volume 1 — Insurance Fundamentals

Volume 1 provides the foundational knowledge on which all underwriting activity rests. It explains what insurance is, the principles that govern insurance contracts, how insurance works as a business, the structure of the market, the legal environment, and the operational functions of an insurer.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this volume you should be able to:

1. Define insurance and explain how risk is transferred and pooled.
2. Explain the six core principles of insurance and apply them to real situations.
3. Describe how an insurer makes money and what a combined ratio is.
4. Describe the participants in the insurance market and how they interact.
5. Identify the legal requirements that shape insurance contracts.
6. Describe the end-to-end operations of an insurer and the role of each function.
