# Volume 4 — Underwriting

Volume 4 is the practical core of the handbook: how insurance risk is analyzed, selected, and priced. It covers the underwriting process, information sources, risk assessment frameworks, risk engineering, the role of the underwriter, line-specific underwriting, and portfolio management.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this volume you should be able to:

1. Describe the underwriting process from submission to issuance.
2. Identify and use the principal information sources.
3. Apply underwriting principles: selection, classification, pricing discipline, and appetite.
4. Assess physical and moral hazard, including COPE.
5. Explain the role of risk engineering in underwriting.
6. Apply line-specific underwriting considerations.
7. Manage an underwriting portfolio: aggregation, concentration, and cycle discipline.
8. Describe how automation and data reshape underwriting.
