# 8.6 Market Participants

## Overview

The US insurance market operates through a **chain of participants** — insurers, producers, brokers, reinsurers, and service providers — each with distinct roles, incentives, and regulatory obligations. Understanding who does what is essential for underwriting, distribution, and claims.

## The Distribution Chain

```
Insurer → Producer/Agent → Broker → Insured
                     ↓
              MGA/MGU, Program Administrators
```

## Insurers

| Type | Role |
|---|---|
| **Admitted carriers** | Licensed in each state; regulated rates/forms |
| **Surplus lines insurers** | Non-admitted; flexible terms for hard-to-place risks |
| **Mutual companies** | Policyholder-owned |
| **Stock companies** | Shareholder-owned |
| **Captives** | Self-insurance vehicles |
| **Risk retention groups** | Member-owned liability insurers |

## Producers

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| **Independent agent** | Represents multiple insurers; owns the customer relationship |
| **Captive/exclusive agent** | Represents one insurer; tied distribution |
| **Broker** | Represents the insured; negotiates placement |
| **Wholesale broker** | Intermediary between retail broker and surplus lines |
| **Managing general agent (MGA)** | Delegated underwriting and policy issuance authority |
| **Managing general underwriter (MGU)** | Underwriting-only delegation |

## MGA vs. Broker

| Aspect | MGA | Broker |
|---|---|---|
| **Represents** | Insurer (underwriting authority) | Insured |
| **Authority** | Bind, issue, underwrite per agreement | Place coverage |
| **Regulation** | Delegated authority model acts | Broker licensing |
| **Compensation** | Commission from insurer | Commission from insured/insurer |

## Reinsurers

| Type | Role |
|---|---|
| **Treaty reinsurers** | Automatic cession under agreement |
| **Facultative reinsurers** | Individual risk placement |
| **Proportional (quota share)** | Share premium and losses |
| **Non-proportional (excess of loss)** | Cover above retention |

Reinsurers provide **capacity** and **stability** to the market.

## Rating Agencies

| Agency | Role |
|---|---|
| **AM Best** | Financial strength ratings for insurers |
| **S&P, Moody's, Fitch** | Credit and financial ratings |

Ratings influence **market confidence, distribution, and reinsurance pricing**.

## Service Providers

| Provider | Service |
|---|---|
| **TPA (Third-Party Administrator)** | Claims administration for self-insureds and insurers |
| **Loss control / risk engineering** | Inspections, surveys, recommendations |
| **Appraisers and experts** | Valuation, causation, forensic services |
| **Attorneys** | Coverage, defense, subrogation |
| **Actuaries** | Pricing, reserving, capital modeling |
| **Technology vendors** | PAS, claims systems, analytics |
| **Software & data providers** | MVRs, scoring, compliance data |

## State and Federal Roles

| Participant | Role |
|---|---|
| **State insurance departments** | License, examine, regulate |
| **NAIC** | Coordination and standards |
| **Courts** | Coverage interpretation, bad faith |
| **Guaranty funds** | Insolvency protection |

## The Insured's Advisors

| Advisor | Role |
|---|---|
| **Risk manager** | Identifies and manages enterprise risk |
| **Agent/broker** | Advises on coverage and placement |
| **Attorney** | Contract review, claims, risk transfer |
| **Consultant** | Program design, captive analysis |

## Market Dynamics

| Force | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Capacity** | Available capital drives pricing |
| **Competition** | New entrants, products, technology |
| **Regulation** | Filing burden, conduct standards |
| **Loss trends** | Social inflation, climate |
| **Technology** | Insurtech, data, AI |

## Summary

- The market has **insurers, producers, brokers, reinsurers, and service providers**.
- **MGAs** hold delegated authority; **brokers** represent the insured.
- **Reinsurers** provide capacity and stability.
- **Rating agencies, regulators, and courts** shape behavior.
- **Market dynamics** — capacity, competition, losses, technology — drive results.
