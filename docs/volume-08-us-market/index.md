# Volume 8 — The US Insurance Market

Volume 8 covers the structure, regulation, and practice of the United States insurance market. It explains the **state-based regulatory system**, the organizations and rating authorities involved, and how underwriting and policy administration operate within the US framework.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this volume you should be able to:

1. Describe the size and structure of the US insurance market.
2. Explain state-based regulation and the NAIC.
3. Understand forms, rate, and rule filing processes.
4. Identify licensing and admission requirements.
5. Work with standard ISO, ACORD, and NCCI forms.
6. Describe the roles of market participants.
