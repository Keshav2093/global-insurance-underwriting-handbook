# 8.5 Key Coverages & Forms

## Overview

The US market relies on **standardized forms** developed by advisory organizations — primarily **ISO** for commercial and personal property/casualty, **NCCI** for workers compensation, and **ACORD** for applications, certificates, and binders. Standard forms create consistency, regulatory efficiency, and a common legal vocabulary.

## The Purpose of Standard Forms

| Benefit | Description |
|---|---|
| **Consistency** | Same wording nationwide; predictable interpretation |
| **Regulatory efficiency** | Pre-filed bureau forms approved at the state level |
| **Marketability** | Insurers can adopt forms quickly with deviations |
| **Education** | Common vocabulary for training and claims |
| **Claims clarity** | Known coverage; fewer disputes |

## ISO (Insurance Services Office)

| Product Area | Forms |
|---|---|
| **Commercial property** | CP 00 01 (Building & Personal Property), CP 10 30 (Causes of Loss) |
| **Commercial general liability** | CG 00 01 (Occurrence), CG 00 02 (Claims-made) |
| **Commercial auto** | CA 00 01 (Business Auto), CA 00 20 (Garage) |
| **Commercial package** | CP 00 90 (Package), CP 00 99 (Condominium) |
| **Personal lines** | HO 00 03 (Special Form), PAP (Personal Auto PP 00 01) |
| **Umbrella/excess** | CU 00 01 (Commercial Umbrella) |

ISO also publishes **rating rules, classifications, and advisory rates**.

## ACORD

**ACORD** (Association for Cooperative Operations Research and Development) standardizes business documents:

| Form Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Applications** | Commercial lines applications |
| **Certificates** | ACORD 25 (Certificate of Liability Insurance) |
| **Binders** | ACORD 75 (Binder) |
| **Evidence of property** | ACORD 27/28 (Evidence of Commercial Property) |
| **Loss runs** | Claims data requests |
| **Data standards** | XML for policy, claims, billing |

ACORD forms are **industry standard** for evidence and submissions.

## NCCI

The **National Council on Compensation Insurance** provides:

- **Workers compensation forms** — WC policy, endorsements.
- **Classification system** — class codes for payroll rating.
- **Experience rating** — E-mod calculation.
- **Advisory rates** and loss costs.
- **State-specific** filings and manuals.

## Other Organizations

| Organization | Role |
|---|---|
| **AAIS** | Forms and data for property, inland marine, specialty |
| **Surety & Fidelity Association (SFAA)** | Surety bond forms and data |
| **State bureaus** | Some states (workers comp) still operate rating bureaus |

## Rating and Classification Systems

| System | Use |
|---|---|
| **ISO classification** | Commercial auto, property, GL classes |
| **NCCI class codes** | Workers comp payroll classes |
| **SIC / NAICS** | Business classification reference |
| **Territory codes** | Geographic rating factors |
| **Schedule rating** | Individual account credits/debits |

## Forms Management

| Control | Practice |
|---|---|
| **Edition control** | Only current approved editions used |
| **State-specific versions** | Correct state variations |
| **Endorsement library** | Complete set of available endorsements |
| **System integration** | Forms tied to policy data |
| **Version history** | Full audit of form changes |

## Form vs. Endorsement

| Element | Form | Endorsement |
|---|---|---|
| **Base document** | Core policy coverage | Amendment to the form |
| **Numbering** | Form series (CP, CG, HO, PP) | Form or letter-number |
| **Effect** | Sets the base terms | Modifies terms |
| **Filing** | Filed and approved | Also filed (state-specific) |

## Summary

- **ISO, ACORD, and NCCI** set the standard forms and structures.
- **Standard forms** bring consistency, efficiency, and legal clarity.
- **Key forms** cover property, liability, auto, umbrella, WC, and crime.
- **Classification systems** (NCCI, ISO, NAICS) support rating.
- **Forms management** — control editions, state versions, and integration.
