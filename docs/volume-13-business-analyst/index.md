# Volume 13 — Insurance Business Analyst

Volume 13 covers the role of the insurance business analyst (BA): how BAs work in P&C insurance organizations, how they support underwriting, claims, product development, and technology projects, and the tools and techniques they use.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this volume you should be able to:

1. Define the insurance BA role and responsibilities.
2. Apply BA techniques in underwriting projects.
3. Support product and rating configuration initiatives.
4. Analyze claims workflows and requirements.
5. Manage requirements through the SDLC.
6. Use tools such as BRDs, process maps, and SQL.
